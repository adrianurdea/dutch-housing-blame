# Dutch Housing Blame

Who gets blamed for the Dutch housing crisis? Comparing Dutch- and English-language Reddit communities (2024–2026).

Group 20 · *The Social Web* · Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, 2026

## Research question
Who do people blame for the Dutch housing crisis online, and do Dutch-language and
English-language communities blame different actors?
